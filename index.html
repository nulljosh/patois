Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. An account keeps your streak, XP and review queue in sync across devices.

Do I need an account?

Not on iPhone or Mac. The web app asks you to sign in with email, Apple or Google.

What is Patois?

The way a place really talks at home, rather than the textbook version. That is how the lessons are written.

Who is Pat?

A scarlet macaw. Parrots learn by repeating what they hear, and so do you.
